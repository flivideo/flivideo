## Script (spoken form, one line = one beat)

- You joined. Now what?
- This is the Start Here path, and it's built for one thing: your first real result, in your first week.
- Most communities hand you a library and wish you luck. We hand you an order.
- Part one: say hello.
- Post in the community. Who you are, your channel, and the one job that eats your week.
- We read every one, and they tell us which skill to build next.
- Part two: the quick win. Eight short lessons, and one skill: Hook Doctor.
- Lesson one: install Claude Code, and Hook Doctor. You do this once.
- Lesson two: run it on your own script.
- Lesson three: read what breaks your hook.
- Lesson four: pick the best of the five new hooks, and use it.
- Lesson five: find a channel whose hooks run long.
- Lesson six: run Hook Doctor on their script.
- Lesson seven: send them the new hooks.
- Lesson eight: post the result in the community.
- At the end, your own hook is fixed, and you've done the kind of work other channels pay for. That's your win.
- Part three: the skill library.
- Thirty-one lessons, for the jobs your channel actually has. Nine of them show you how to sell what they make.
- Plus over thirty prompt packs, and the recipe library.
- Part four: the deep end.
- The e-book, on systematizing faceless YouTube videos. Read it once your first result is in, not before.
- Part five, last on purpose: the slow stuff.
- Your own community. An email list. Your own product.
- Those pay off over months. We're building ours right now, so you can watch how it goes.
- Why this order? Because one skill you actually used beats ten you downloaded.
- Win first. Library second. Slow things last.
- Start Here is the first course in the classroom. Open it, post your hello, and do lesson one today.
