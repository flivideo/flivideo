Hear your script, one paragraph at a time

Today you can only read the Story Pilot script. The idea: a small audio file for each paragraph, played in order across your chapters like FliHub's auto-next, so you can listen to a draft and only redo the paragraph you changed.

Sample: D13 Overlay Pilot script (26 paragraphs · 6 chapters) · Status: design only, not built · Full write-up: docs/specs/storypilot-listen-2026-11-01.md

Where it hurts

You write a story script and have to read it yourself to judge it. "If I wanted to change some words somewhere, I wouldn't have to wait a long time." Regenerating the whole thing for one edit would be exactly that wait.

The spec

What the job is

Cache unitOne audio file per paragraph: an edit redoes one small file.
Play unitChapters (from the script's chapter list). The bar lists them; auto-next runs paragraph to paragraph across chapter boundaries; "replay this chapter" plays just its paragraphs.
StaleThe paragraph's text hash no longer matches the saved one. Whitespace-only edits don't count.
First timeAudio is made in the background when the script is first written. Never blocks the save.
After thatEdits only mark "out of date". You click to regenerate one, or all stale.
Inserting a paragraphMatched by text first, so the ones after it are not redone.
PlayerOne audio element; when a paragraph ends it starts the next (same as FliHub's auto-next); remembers your auto-next choice.
CleanupSuperseded files are deleted as soon as a new one is made. The whole folder is also cleared by Clear renders.
Who speaksA new FliTools "speak" verb runs Kokoro; Story Pilot never calls Python.

Who owns what

  • Player + manifest + stale logic: Story Pilot module (storypilot-dev)
  • Speech: FliTools (new verb)
  • File serving to the browser: FliStudio core (not checked)
  • fli-core contract: no change

Out of scope

  • Auto-clear when a video is published — a separate decision for all render caches
  • Voice cloning, cloud voices

What I checked, and what I didn't
