## Interpreted
### Essence
A coach working next to you, narrating what the screen is doing and why.

### Tone (1-10)
Formal-Casual 7 · Serious-Funny 4 · Respectful-Irreverent 4 · Matter-of-fact-Enthusiastic 5.

### Signature moves (verbatim)
- Problem-question open: "Do you love the idea of the BMAD method? The ability to generate a massive specification for an application…" (-GKpGdonMbg)
- Series recap open: "It's the 12 days of Claude and we are up to day nine of Vibe coding using Claude code little applications and tools…" (2jMdt3U4AkA)
- Imagine-if open: "Imagine building a self-editing application." (zfOFf6wMfh0)

- Sign-off: "I'm AppyDave. Please like and subscribe. I'll see you in the next video." (4N36BvvqNxE, FcPrU_fr-0A)
- Honest side-note intro: "Now that was just a quick side quest for me in my video creation process." (kn_54J1fxec outro)

### Anti-voice
Not found in the sample: hype adjectives ("insane", "game-changer"); growth-hacking language. Not measured beyond these videos. TODO(David): confirm.

### Cadence
Sentences 9-17 words (store medians), run-on explaining sentences joined by "and"; hedges ("some sort of", "maybe", "I think"); frequent restating of what was done yesterday.

## Fidelity checklist
- [ ] "we" used >= 30 per 1,000 words
- [ ] at least one "a little bit" / "have a look at" / "what we'll do is" per 2 minutes
- [ ] opens with a question, an imagine-if or a series recap, never a greeting alone
- [ ] sign-off names "Happy Dave" and "see you in the next video"
- [ ] zero hype adjectives
