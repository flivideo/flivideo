1 · FliStudio live at :7150

  1. Open D13 → Notes in the left rail. Look for three records: your ask (marked Done), the script change with your "because" words and before/after in red and green, and the open→done line.
  2. Press ⌥⇧N (or the speech-bubble icon, top right). Things that can take a note get an outline as you hover: the project name, rail rows, files, resources, launch pieces, Story Pilot paragraphs.
  3. Click a Story Pilot paragraph, type a note, Save. Do it again with This is an ask on. The Notes count in the rail shows open asks only.
  4. In Notes, press Mark done on that ask. Use the filters: Open asks · Notes · Changes.
  5. Press ⌥⇧N again. Notes mode is off and clicks work normally.

2 · FliCut start it first

  1. Start FliCut as usual and open a cut. The header has a ✎ Notes button, off.
  2. Press ⌥⇧N (⌘⇧N works here too). Hover words, cuts, recordings, the player. Click a word, write a note, Save.
  3. Do another with the ask switch on. Open list (bottom right) and press ✓ Done.
  4. Make a cut, then undo. Each one writes a change record into the project's fli.notes.jsonl. They aren't shown in FliCut's small list, only in FliStudio's Notes view.
