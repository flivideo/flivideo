What Mike's new video changes

Compared with the existing analysis, built on his other two videos. Full note

  • Holds up: speaking rate (191 wpm), 11-word sentences, honesty brakes, plant and payoff, no hype words.
  • Weakened: "barely uses questions" is not his general pattern; this video asks 21. It also has no trace-back reveal and no frame flip at the close.
  • New: Mike has a third format, a chaptered tutorial with one real bug carried to a real PR.

How far to trust this

  • Old AITLDR and Maitis: openings and closings were read closely, the middles less so.
  • AppyDave has no planned scripts, and its project recordings couldn't give a per-video speaking rate.
  • Mike's story format rests on 2 videos.
  • This is a worker's analysis. I checked the headers and spot-checked some numbers; I didn't re-derive them all.
