HyperFrames vs FliEdit — one composition, both editors

The d01 trial (two takes, two DJI cutaways, a callout, a music bed that ducks) built once in HyperFrames and once in FliEdit, both rendered at 1920×1080, 25 fps, 65.12 s. Run 2026-11-04 on the M4. No FliEdit code was changed.

Verdict: FliEdit assembled everything it has verbs for, and was faster to render; two of the six steps cannot be done in FliEdit at all. Cutaways, take order, timing and the music bed matched frame-for-frame. The callout and the duck are real gaps (H3 text layer, H1 volume automation). Gaps are recorded, not worked around.
