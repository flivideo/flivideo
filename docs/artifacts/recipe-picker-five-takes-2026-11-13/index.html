Five takes on one screen

The screen where you choose what a script is built from. Five different ideas, each a working mock on the same real data: 46 guides, 6 styles, 30 offered for a long video, old copies included.

How to use this page
  1. Open a mock, click around in it, come back.
  2. Tap a verdict and say what you like, dislike, or want to ask. It saves as you go, and Claude can read it.
  3. At the bottom, pick one or name a mix.
01

One sentence, with change

Open the mock ↗

The recipe reads as one sentence that is already right. The bold words are the only controls.

It bets that you almost never change these, so reading is instant and choosing sits two clicks deep. It gives up: you cannot see the whole library from here.

http://localhost:7421/designs/01-recipe-picker-settled-line/

02

Pick the family

Open the mock ↗

You pick one style and get its four guides as a set. Swap a single part only when you want a mix.

It bets that the real decision is whose kind of video this is, made once. It gives up: the six style cards are always on screen, even on an ordinary day.

http://localhost:7421/designs/01-recipe-picker-style-first/

03

The video as a drawing

Open the mock ↗

The script is drawn from its real word counts and chapters. The four parts are tracks under it, like a video editor.

It bets that you think in the shape of a video and want to see where a guide acts on it. It gives up: a new script has nothing to draw yet.

http://localhost:7421/designs/01-recipe-picker-drawn-shape/

04

No picker, only questions

Open the mock ↗

The page says Ready and shows only what the engine could not decide. An answer can be made permanent.

It bets that the choices are a mess to clean up once, not a decision to make every video. It gives up: you only meet other styles if you go looking.

http://localhost:7421/designs/01-recipe-picker-ask-only/

05

The whole library as a grid

Open the mock ↗

Every guide sits in a grid of styles by parts. Your recipe is a line drawn through it.

It bets that you are not asking for less power. You want to see all of it in one look. It gives up: it is the densest of the five.

http://localhost:7421/designs/01-recipe-picker-shelf-map/

Your pick

Pick one, or say which pieces to combine. Nothing gets built for real until you say so.
