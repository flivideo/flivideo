Story guides: the shelf above the islands

Document Islands answer what happened. Story guides answer how to tell it: verbal style, voice, structure. Guides need two keys, level and type, and a build should pick them by what kind of video it is.

Verdict: the d13 launch stamp didn't come from a wrong guide but from a flat list. Story Pilot hands every video the same verbal-style docs, whatever the form. The guide was written mostly from tutorials, so a story video inherited tutorial bookends. The fix is in how guides are organised, not in better wording of the Global/Brand boxes.

04How a build should pick guides

Each guide carries two tags: type, and applies to (form: story or tutorial; length: short or long). A build knows the video's form and length, walks the levels from video up to global, and keeps only the guides that match.
