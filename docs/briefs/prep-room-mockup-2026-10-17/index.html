The Prep Room

Every video project gets the same prep pages, in the same order, in one place in FliStudio. Tap a filled slot to preview it. Switch between D07, D09 and D11 to see which slots each video actually filled.

"Having them in a cohesive order, knowing when to use them, and consistency across 10 videos would be useful."

Open questions for David

  1. Own menu item or inside Resources? The mock gives Prep its own row, like Launch. My take: own row, so it is one tap away on recording day.
  2. Are these the right five? Recording Kit, Outline + Script, Outcomes, Presenter, What Got Built. D11 had no job list, so Outcomes and Presenter would be lighter there.
  3. Is a preview card enough? FliStudio can show a summary and an Open button. Showing the whole page inside FliStudio is harder, because claude.ai pages do not load inside other apps.
  4. Borrowed pages: D09 reuses D07's What Got Built for its recap. Show a borrowed page in the slot (as here, marked "from D07") or only under extras?
  5. Empty slots on day one? Should every new project open with the five slots already showing, empty, with who fills each?

What is real: page titles, links and dates come from ARTIFACTS.md and the D07 and D09 fli.resources.json files. The menu and styles copy FliStudio's client code (tokens.css, nav.css, the Resources panel).

What is illustrative: the small sketches in each preview (drawn, not screenshots), the Prep menu item and its counts, the slot names, and the "who fills it" lines. Nothing in FliStudio has changed.
