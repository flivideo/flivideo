## Added later on 2026-10-06 (David using Launch on D02) — notes only, Launch/Publish still ON HOLD

11. **The ⓘ icons make the page shake.** Hovering one makes the whole page jitter left and right by about 10–12 px, over and over (full screen, Chrome). Likely cause: the tooltip briefly overflows, so a scrollbar appears, the layout shifts, the hover is lost, the scrollbar goes, and it repeats. Not verified.
12. **The title pool (left column) doesn't explain itself.** 26 ranked titles with scores, "+19 more", and clicking a row seems to do nothing. Inferred from the screenshots, not checked in code: clicking a title and then a thumbnail selects them for "Pick a title and a thumbnail" (building your own pair). Nothing on screen says so.
13. **"Test winner" is a trap.** David clicked it before any test had run because he didn't know what it meant. It made pair A's thumbnail "chosen" (05:08:50Z). Undo isn't offered, and "Remove" then took only the title out of the test (05:08:55Z), leaving the thumbnail chosen. The result is a half-state: one thumbnail chosen while pairs B and C are still in test. Needs: label "Mark winner (after the YouTube test)", disabled until the video is published, an undo, and Remove that takes out both halves of a pair or says what it does.
14. **Undo + who did it, across every app.** Each change already records `human:ui` vs `agent:<name>` (the `changed.by` stamp in fli.resources.json). David: that's as much identity as there will be, and it's enough. But saves happen everywhere (undo stacks, state files) and no app shows them or lets you undo them. Expect this to bite more often. A future cross-app design topic, not a fix.
15. **The Launch page is hard to read.** It's dense, the "why" toggles repeat on every row, Tuber's reasoning is shown in full, and the 2,255-character description sits in a narrow column.
16. **"Tuber is waiting for your decision here" doesn't say what to do.** The Approve button is at the bottom, out of sight. David found it by scrolling. The banner should name the action ("Read it, then Approve or edit").
17. **The "Cuddy ⚠" tag.** The warning asks "a misspelling on purpose?" and gives the user nothing to act on; there's no remove-this-tag control. Tuber's note says it's deliberate (how the transcript hears "Cutty").
18. **Re-pairing is not intuitive.** Select a title, then a thumbnail, then "Shortlist this pair", with no instructions. The original pairings (which title went with which thumbnail) aren't shown anywhere once a pair is removed.
19. **"Shortlist this pair" doesn't clear a "winner" mark.** After David re-added pair A, its thumbnail stayed `chosen` while the title went back `in-test`.
20. **The end of the flow is a dead end for a human.** No copy buttons, no "show in Finder" for the video to upload, no single upload kit. The "Fill YouTube Studio" agent isn't built. Stopgap used on D02: `videos/cutty-audio-cleanup/upload-kit.txt`, written by orch from fli.resources.json.
21. **Chapters may be off.** Tuber made the timestamps from the pre-edit recording lengths (5:52 total, its note says "PRE-EDIT"); the final video is 5:36. "Chapters fit the final video's length" only checks the last chapter starts before the end.
22. **The Cuts list shows the wrong length.** For D02 it shows 5:36, which is the kept clips summed straight from the saved file with no padding. The export FliCut actually plans and produces is 5:44.5: `plannedDuration` 344.48 in last-arms.json, and the export probes at 344.5 s. The difference is the 0.15 s padding at each edge of the kept clips (ADR-0002), plus snapping. David took the correct export for the wrong video because of this. The list should show the export length.

## After D02 was published (David, 2026-10-06 evening): the Launch → YouTube round trip

23. **Launch and Publish are split at the wrong point.** The YouTube link comes back while you're filling in YouTube fields, but "Mark published" lives on Publish. The link box belongs at the end of the upload flow.
24. **Launch needs an upload WIZARD.** David: "next, next, next", one field at a time with its copy, folder or path button, so he can get to the next value quickly and drop it into the YouTube form. The long scrolling board is "too much, too far away".
25. **The page mixes two jobs.** DECIDING (review Tuber's proposals, approve, pick pairs) and DOING (copying values into YouTube Studio). The board suits deciding; doing needs the wizard. Proposal: Launch has two modes, "Decide" (today's board) and "Upload to YouTube" (the wizard), and the wizard opens only when 7/7 are decided.
26. **The wizard follows YouTube Studio's own order:** upload file → title → description → thumbnail / Test & Compare (3 titles + 3 thumbnails) → playlists → audience → tags → language and category → subtitles (.srt, With timing) → end screen → visibility → paste the YouTube link (mark published) → after it goes live: post and pin the comment. The page's order today (pairs, description, chapters, tags, pinned comment, end screen, settings) matches nothing in YouTube.
27. **Stale yellow banners.** "tuber is waiting for your decision here: D02 … r2 ready" still shows directly above "You approved this. By you." on description, chapters, tags and end screen. The gate didn't close, or the banner ignores the approval. This one is a bug.
28. **Copy icons with no label.** Tags vs hashtags are two identical icons side by side, and so are the end-screen title vs URL. Each icon needs a word ("copy tags", "copy URL").
29. **`>_` means "copy path" on Launch but "terminal" in the header.** One glyph, two meanings. Use a distinct copy-path icon or label.
30. **Settings values are hidden.** "Category, playlists, audience" shows only a collapsed "Brand defaults (4)"; the values to type into YouTube aren't visible or copyable without expanding.
31. **Chapters appear twice.** Once inside the description and once as their own piece. In the wizard, chapters ride inside the description; the separate piece is for review only.
32. **Resources pile up.** RESOURCES 22: 3 descriptions, 2 pinned comments, 2 chapters, 3 end screens. Superseded r1 proposals stay counted, which reads as clutter or duplicates.
33. **"Test winner" / "Remove" still sit on every pair** before the video is even live (see #13).
34. **The project dashboard** (trash, renders, five app cards, resources) still sits above Launch and pushes the work down (see #1).
35. **Angles before rendering (from Tuber, G6).** YLO asks David to accept the 3 angles (story + title) BEFORE thumbnails render, because rendering costs his ChatGPT session. Launch has no place for that decision: packaging can't be approved with no thumbnails. For now Buzz owns it (decision card "draw these?"), and packaging is approved on Launch once the thumbnails exist. Design question for the Decide half of the redesign: should "Angles" become step 0 on the page?
36. **Suggestions after approval need a visible signal.** The D02 r2 SEO pass arrived after approval and was invisible once the banner fix landed. Show a quiet "newer suggestion: compare" on approved pieces; don't block and don't hide.
37. **FliCut Remember bar: say what it will merge into.** "Fixed 'Api Dave' → 'AppyDave'. Remember: VIDEO / APPYDAVE / GLOBAL" made David worry it would create a duplicate AppyDave. It doesn't: `merge: true` adds the spelling to the existing entry, and levels union their heard-as lists. But the bar should say so, e.g. "adds 'Api Dave' to AppyDave (already knows: Abhi Dave, Appy Dave, Happy Dave)", and say which level already holds the term.
38. **The e2e drill fails at Publish `check-video`, before it reaches Launch.** It fails the same way on the commit before today's Launch work, so it's an older problem, but it means the Launch steps aren't covered end to end. Needs someone to look at Publish's check-video in e2e/drill.spec.ts.
