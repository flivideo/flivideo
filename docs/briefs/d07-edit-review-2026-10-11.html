ACutting a word leaves part of it behind biggest new problem

You saw
Eight times: you could still hear we, and (three times), after, in, and so, plus a click where and had been. Cutting so left oh, so you undid it. Words like "And", "But" and "So" at the start of a sentence, the ones you most want gone, are the ones that won't go cleanly. The fine-tune panel has never once fixed one for you.
The file shows
Your 11:58 cut of "And" in 03-1. The transcriber says "And" lasts 0.10 s (25.18–25.28), and your cut removed exactly that. The 0.22 s just before it has no silence in it and stays in. That's where the rest of the "and" is. Short words get word boxes that are too tight, so cutting the box leaves the edges of the sound. 19 of your cuts in this edit are 0.35 s or shorter, so each one is a suspect.
Fix
When you cut a word, widen the cut out to the silence on either side (the same "snap to silence" pauses already get), and play the join straight away so you hear it at once. Later: a one-click sweep that drops a leading "And / But / So".

BDead air you can't get rid of medium

You saw
About 4 s of blank between the 4.7 s and 0.8 s pauses (end of 02-2 into 04). The edges wouldn't widen, and splitting didn't help. It's almost like I've got to use a totally different video editor.
The file shows
The pause cut starts where "without" ends (45.65 s), but the kept piece before it runs on to 48.03 s. When kept and cut overlap, kept wins, so about 2.4 s of near-silence plays. Then 0.5 s more at the start of 03-1, where the kept piece starts inside the 0.8 s cut. That's about 2.9 s of dead air in all. This is the worst spot in the whole edit; the next worst is 0.8 s in 06-4. The likely cause is two tiny sounds (0.10 s and 0.02 s) inside the silence, each counted as speech, so the kept piece stretches out to cover them. I haven't confirmed why the edges wouldn't move.
Fix
Treat a sound under about 0.1 s inside a silence as silence. Let the fine-tune drag a pause's edge into kept material when you mean it.

CGrammar is a different job from spelling medium

You saw
  • A capital and full stop left behind after a cut.
  • "A .I." instead of "AI".
  • Is "we can do different versions" the end of a sentence?
  • Should there be a comma before "but"? Between "Claude.md" and "JSON"?
  • "parsers" → "passes" is a mishearing, not a spelling mistake, but the fix treats it like one.
  • plugin, plug-in or plugins: you weren't sure.
A lot of the time I'm editing for things that are really just grammar being written incorrectly.
The file shows
You chose three different spellings in 75 seconds: "plug-ins" (12:16:42), "plugin" (12:17:50), "plugins" (12:17:57). That's the missing agreed spelling showing up in your own hands. If "Remember" were pressed on parsers → passes, it would change "parsers" everywhere, which is wrong.
Fix
Split "Fix words" three ways. Spelling can be remembered. Misheard applies to this spot only and is never remembered. Punctuation changes text only, never audio. Tidying grammar can be a pass after the cut, because it only matters where text is shown: captions and descriptions.
