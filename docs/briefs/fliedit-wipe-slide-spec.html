Overlays can wipe or slide in, not only fade

Today an image or B-roll on the overlay track can only fade in and out. You've never seen a wipe or a slide in FliEdit, because there isn't one: this page shows what they would look like, and asks the five calls that decide how they feel.

Sample: d06 resources/project/d06-b4-v3-final.png, 1280×720, on O1Size: ~3 h for one coderStatus: spec only, not built

Where it hurts

1Cut in FliCut, open in FliEditToday: works. The cut goes on V1, the image on O1.

3Export and trust itToday: the fade is proven, preview = export, by a golden test. Anything new must pass the same test.

The spec

What the job is

Two new kindsAn overlay clip's in-edge and out-edge can be fade (today), wipe or slide. Each edge is chosen separately.
WipeThe card is revealed by a straight edge moving across it. It never moves; only how much of it shows changes.
SlideThe card travels in from outside the frame to where you placed it, fully opaque the whole way.
DirectionWipe: left→right, right→left, top→bottom, bottom→top. Slide: from left, right, top or bottom edge. Defaults per Choices below.
LengthIn frames, like the fade. Can't be longer than the clip (same refusal the fade gives).
Where you set itInspector, next to the fade in / fade out buttons: a small kind picker per edge. Agents: transition.set with kind: "wipe" | "slide" and direction.
Between two clipsRefused: wipe and slide are edge-only. Joins stay dissolve or cut.
Old editsOpen unchanged. A fade stays a fade, pixel for pixel.

Out of scope

  • Wipe/slide between two touching clips on one track (ffmpeg xfade). A separate job if wanted.
  • Ken Burns pan/zoom and keyframed moves — the brief's next "nice to have".
  • The cutaway plan's fade field (edit-ops.ts:425-455) stays fade-only for now.
  • Soft-edged wipes, push, zoom, spin.

What I checked, and what I didn't

  • One renderer for preview and export. src/core/render/plan.ts plan() builds the ffmpeg graph; stillArgs (:189, preview frame), chunkArgs (:235, playback), proxyArgs (:224) and exportArgs (:203) all call it. So the new kinds are written once and preview can't drift from export.
  • How fade works today. plan.ts:68-69 turns a one-sided fade transition into a fade-in/out time; plan.ts:126-127 adds ffmpeg fade=…:alpha=1 to the overlay's chain (linear). The overlay is drawn at a fixed spot: plan.ts:131 overlay=x=…:y=… from geometry.ts:15-33. Slide makes that x/y a per-frame expression; wipe adds a per-frame mask on the chain.
  • Model and verb. schema.ts:130 TransitionKind = ['dissolve','fade']; schema.ts:132-141 has no direction or easing field. edit-ops.ts:390-418 setTransition validates; capabilities/timeline.ts:122-126 exposes transition.set. Inspector fade buttons: Inspector.tsx:58-59, 115-118.
  • Golden parity tests exist and are the template. test/golden/overlay-image.test.ts (image on O1, fade in/out, preview = export at 5 frames, colour probe, negative control) and test/golden/parity.test.ts (d04, dissolve + PiP). Run by vitest.golden.config.ts.
  • Real data. d06 has d06-b4-v3-final.png, d06-b5-compact-final.png, d06-b6-v3-final.png in resources/project/; B4 measured 1280×720 with sips.
  • Not checked: that a per-frame expression gives the same pixel in a one-frame preview window as in the full export. plan() shifts time per window (setpts=PTS-…, plan.ts:128), so the slide/wipe must be written in timeline frames, not window time. If the bundled ffmpeg evaluates overlay x/y or the mask differently per window, the job grows by a spike. No code was run.

Choices for David

1 · Wipe direction (default)

Left→right, right→left, top→bottom, bottom→top. All four will exist; this picks what you get without choosing.

Left→right. It reads like text and like a reveal; the other three stay one click away.

2 · Slide edge (default)

From the nearest frame edge to where the card sits (automatic), or a fixed edge: right, left, top, bottom.

Nearest edge. Your cards sit top-right in a corner more often than not, so that's "from the right" here, and a card placed bottom-left slides up from the bottom without you thinking about it.

3 · Duration (default)

1 s (25 frames, same as the fade button today), 0.5 s (snappier), or 0.75 s.

Keep 1 s for wipe, 0.5 s for slide. A slide is a move, and moves feel slow sooner than fades do; a 1 s slide drags.

4 · Easing

Linear (like the fade today), or ease-out in / ease-in out (fast start, soft landing), as in the demo.

Ease-out in, ease-in out, for wipe and slide only. Linear motion looks robotic. Fade stays linear so its existing goldens don't move.

5 · Does the out mirror the in?

Mirror: the card leaves the way it came (slides back out right; wipe retracts right→left). Or continue: it carries on (exits left; wipe sweeps off to the right).

Mirror. It reads as "tucked away", which suits a cutaway that returns you to the presenter. In and out are separate edges anyway, so you can still set any other pairing.
