## Titles

- Option 1: I Automated Myself as a Human YouTuber
- Option 2: My Content Creator Apps + the AI Agents That Drive Them
- Option 3: No Avatars, No Editing: How I Automate My YouTube

## Premise
Most videos about automating content use AI avatars and generative AI. This one covers automating the work of a *human* YouTuber, using a suite of small apps David built (FliVideo) and agents that drive them.

## Who it's for
YouTubers and solo creators who film themselves and want less time in editing. It also suits builders who like "build in public" agent and tooling content.

## The promise
You'll see the full recording pipeline: plan the project, record, rename and keep good takes, get transcripts automatically, ask an agent about the video, record screencasts with automatic zooms, and use a teleprompter. It's all his own software, and agents can drive every part of it.
