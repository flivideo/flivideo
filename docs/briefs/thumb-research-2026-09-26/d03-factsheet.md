# d03 — Cutty presenter tracking (AppyDave) — video fact sheet

**Status: recorded.** 8 takes and a short script, about 550 words, so probably 3–4 minutes. There is no launch pack and no thumbnail work yet. This video follows d02 in the Cutty series.

## Titles
- **Working title:** Cutty presenter tracking: AI that knows where your face is
- Option 1: I Taught AI Where My Face Is (So Graphics Never Cover It)
- Option 2: North, South, East, West: AI That Frames the Presenter
- Option 3: This Agent Finds Safe Space for Graphics Around You

## Premise
Cutty, David's editing agent, watches a video, detects the presenter's head frame by frame, and draws north/south/east/west quadrants that move with them. Overlays and graphics can then go where they won't cover the presenter.

## Who it's for
Creators who add overlays and graphics, and anyone curious about agent-driven editing. It follows on from d02.

## The promise
You'll see an agent that works out automatically where the graphics can go around a moving presenter. This is the groundwork for overlays placed "on autopilot".
